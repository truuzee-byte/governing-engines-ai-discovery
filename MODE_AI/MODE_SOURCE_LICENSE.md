## 7. Exactly 1% of attributable net profit

For each agreed reporting period, Licensee owes the original creator **exactly 1% (one percent)** of **Attributable Net Profit** from Covered Commercial Products:

`Participation Payment = 0.01 × Attributable Net Profit`

`Attributable Net Profit = max(0, Attributable Revenue − Allowable Attributable Expenses)`

The total participation rate is 1%, not 1% separately for each attribution name, original file, modification, license version or collection agent. This is not a percentage of gross revenue, a royalty on unrelated business activity, or company equity. No positive attributable net profit means a zero participation payment, subject to agreed truthful reporting. The participation payment itself is not deducted when calculating its own base, avoiding a circular reduction of the stated rate. No minimum payment, interest, penalty, additional percentage or payment threshold is supplied by this draft.

## 8. Attributable Revenue and allocation

**Attributable Revenue** is the portion of revenue from a Covered Commercial Product economically attributable to its covered code use, determined under the accounting and allocation schedule approved in D03–D06. It is not all earnings of a licensee, affiliate or customer. Genuine refunds, returns, discounts and customer-collected transaction taxes must be treated consistently under that schedule, without both reducing revenue and claiming the same item as an expense.

For a product entirely based on Covered Code, the covered-product profit measure still excludes unrelated activities and allows agreed expenses. For a mixed product/bundle, Licensee must separately identify the covered feature, service or use and apply a reasonable, documented, consistent economic allocation using the approved methodology. D04 must decide when the whole covered product's net profit is the appropriate base versus a justified component allocation; the draft does not authorize a licensee to reduce the base unilaterally to an arbitrary code-value fraction. This text does not choose a universal revenue fraction, presume that 100% of a mixed product is attributable, or authorize one party to change allocations unilaterally. A change in method requires documented agreement under the activation schedule. Related-party arrangements must not artificially shift attributable profits outside the base; consolidated treatment, arm's-length allocation and avoidance of double counting must be settled in D04/D06.

The proposed deductible classes are actual, documented, reasonable costs of earning Attributable Revenue: direct engineering and operational labor; provider/API, hosting, compute and storage charges; product-specific sales/distribution/payment-processing costs; customer support, maintenance and delivery costs; and an evidenced proportionate share of genuinely supporting common expenses. Only the attributable portion may be deducted, once. Normal compensation for actual work is distinguished from a shareholder distribution or an invented charge.

Unrelated business expenses, dividends/owner distributions, capital-raising proceeds or distributions, artificial related-party charges, unsupported allocations and the participation payment itself are not deductible under this proposed definition. Independently owned contribution costs may be deductible when actually incurred for attributable work; ownership does not transfer by deducting an expense.

**Operator decision D06 is required** on the final deduction policy: shared-overhead method, reasonable owner compensation, development/R&D treatment, capital costs and depreciation/amortization, financing costs, transaction versus income taxes, acquisition costs, prior-period losses and carryforwards, related-party charges and any cross-product netting.
