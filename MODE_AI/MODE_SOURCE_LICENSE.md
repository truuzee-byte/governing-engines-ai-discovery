No deduction is allowed for unrelated activity, disguised owner distributions, fabricated expenses, artificial related-party charges, artificial transfers designed to suppress profit, or the MODE royalty itself. This is a contractual product operating-profit calculation, not a company-wide tax-return profit measure. Financing/income-tax/acquisition charges unrelated to operating the covered product do not reduce the base.

Legitimate documented unused operating losses for a covered product carry forward **only to that same product's future profits**. Opening loss balances must be supported by that product's records and must not include losses already recovered or deducted elsewhere. Apply unused loss balances before calculating its next positive royalty base; consume each loss once and report opening/used/closing balances. New operating losses add to that product's balance. No carryback refund of prior paid royalties is created. Another product's losses cannot reduce this product's royalty. Consolidating a statement does not consolidate unrelated profits/losses or permit double deductions.
