Abstract
AI systems now perform functions that were once concentrated inside human research practice:
discovery, retrieval, comparison, synthesis, hypothesis generation, experimentation,
interpretation, and revision. The outward artifact can remain familiar - a paper, report, review, or
finding - while the location of inquiry underneath it changes. This paper investigates that
transition. It distinguishes the research artifact from the inquiry that produced it; develops
Research Custody, Epistemic Materiality, Progression Bias, Oversight Compression, Public
Research Surface, and Semantic Continuity of Inquiry as analytical concepts; and compares
contemporary literature systems, research agents, laboratory systems, intelligence methods, legal
and forensic tools, qualitative-analysis platforms, provenance standards, and emerging epistemic-
state architectures. The investigation repeatedly narrows its own novelty claims: provenance,
contradiction handling, competing hypotheses, multimodal evidence, belief revision, reasoning
provenance, and state transition semantics all have substantial prior art. The remaining design
problem is primarily integrative. The paper proposes an implementation-level architecture in
which the evolving Inquiry - rather than the document, search result, experiment, or final answer
- becomes the persistent primary object. Under Reality Primacy, observations remain
distinguishable from interpretations, representations remain traceable to sources, material
contradictions may remain unresolved, claims are actively challenged, and material state changes
retain evidence, rationale, custody, and proportionate independent adjudication when the
challenged state is material. The paper ends with a prospective Research-Custody Disclosure
applied to itself, explicit limitations and falsification conditions, and a bounded constructive claim:
an integrated, broadly accessible investigator/research system can be organized around
reconstructable semantic continuity of inquiry. The architecture remains a design finding until
implemented and tested.
Executive Summary
The central question is not whether AI can help with research. It already can. The harder question
is what happens when AI increasingly performs material epistemic functions while the
conventional research artifact still looks as though a continuous human researcher performed the
inquiry.
When machines increasingly perform the search and humans
increasingly judge the result, what exactly remains human about the
act we still call research?
This paper does not answer that question by protecting a fixed division of labor. Human beings
are fallible, machines are changing quickly, and many research functions can legitimately move.
Instead, the paper asks whether custody over material inquiry transitions remains identifiable,
inspectable, challengeable, and revisable.
The comparative investigation finds that most local mechanisms already exist somewhere. Elicit
provides structured systematic-review screening and extraction.[2] Scite classifies citation
statements as supporting, contrasting, or mentioning.[3] ResearchRabbit traverses citation

PART I - THE PROBLEM
1. The Mask AI Research Wears
Research has traditionally been legible through artifacts. A paper names authors, presents
methods, cites sources, reports results, and states conclusions. Those conventions do useful work.
They make claims attributable and inspectable. But they also create an assumption: that the
visible artifact is a reasonable map of where the inquiry happened.
AI destabilizes that assumption. A person may formulate a question while a machine identifies
terminology, searches hundreds of sources, selects material, compares findings, proposes a
hypothesis, writes code, runs an experiment, interprets results, drafts a conclusion, and revises
the argument. The final paper may still carry the same familiar structure. The visible form has not
changed nearly as much as the distribution of epistemic work underneath it.
The danger is not machine participation itself. The danger is that research retains its outward
form while custody over material epistemic transitions becomes difficult to locate. A disclosure
that says only “AI was used” may be almost useless. It does not tell the reader whether AI
corrected grammar, discovered the central evidence, generated the causal interpretation, or
decided that contradiction had been resolved.
Research artifact is not inquiry
A paper is not the inquiry. A citation is not the evidentiary judgment that made a source material.
An answer is not the search process that made the answer possible. Publication is a representation
of a larger process in which questions, evidence, interpretations, and hypotheses change state.
The difference becomes consequential when the process is machine-mediated. If the process
remains largely invisible, the reader sees the output but not the movement that gave the output
epistemic authority.
The quiet reassignment of the researcher
The traditional researcher role bundles many functions: question formation, source discovery,
source selection, evidentiary judgment, hypothesis generation, experiment design, contradiction
handling, interpretation, revision, and publication. AI can now perform material portions of
nearly every item on that list. OpenAI describes Deep Research as an agentic system that plans and
executes a multi-step trajectory, browses uploaded files and the web, backtracks as necessary, and
returns a cited report.[5] FutureHouse describes Robin as integrating novel hypothesis generation
with experimental data analysis in a continuous workflow.[6]
The relevant unit is therefore not percentage of machine-generated words. It is movement of
epistemic function.

PART II - RESEARCH CUSTODY
2. Research Custody
Research Custody names responsibility over material inquiry transitions. It is not authorship,
ownership, or truth. A human can retain custody and still be wrong. A machine can perform a
material research operation without being authorized to accept the resulting interpretation.
Custody asks a narrower question: who or what controlled the transition?
Question custody
Questions are not neutral containers. They define what evidence counts, what boundaries appear
relevant, and which alternatives remain invisible. Serious inquiry sometimes discovers that the
original question is itself defective. A system that preserves only answers can therefore lose one of
the most important intellectual transitions: the point at which the inquiry changes what it is
asking.
Evidentiary burden
Finding sources and deciding that evidence is sufficient are different acts. Retrieval systems can
retrieve enormous corpora. They do not thereby acquire authority to declare the matter resolved.
Evidentiary burden concerns the threshold required for a claim, interpretation, hypothesis, or
finding to move to a stronger state.

Contradiction custody
Contradiction creates a decisive test. When credible evidence conflicts, who notices it, preserves it,
decides whether it is material, determines whether progression should halt, and chooses what
additional evidence is required? The system that performs synthesis may be structurally biased
toward continuation even when unresolved conflict is the most faithful representation of the
evidence.
Directional revision
Research direction itself can change. A literature question can become an archival question. A
correlational explanation can become a mechanism question. A technical issue can require a
physical test. Custody therefore extends beyond findings into the decision about what the inquiry
does next.
3. Epistemic Materiality
Not every machine action deserves identical disclosure. A spelling correction and a generated
causal interpretation are both AI contributions, but they are not epistemically equivalent.
Materiality therefore cannot remain a synonym for subjective importance; it must determine
which state changes require preservation, escalation, custody, and disclosure.
Counterfactual materiality test
A contribution, object, or transition is epistemically material when removing it, reversing it, or
materially altering it - while holding the rest of the current inquiry state fixed - could produce a
validly different downstream research state. The relevant downstream state includes the
governing question, admissible evidence set, active hypothesis set, interpretation, explanatory
model or ontology, contradiction status, finding state, research direction, or publication claim and
scope.

The four levels are not claims about truth and are not a substitute for evidence quality. They are

materiality is determined by counterfactual consequence to inquiry state, not by rhetoric, novelty,
or actor preference.
4. Progression Bias
Progression Bias is proposed here as the structural tendency to convert unresolved states into
actionable continuation. It is a testable conceptual hypothesis, not an established law. The
mechanism matters because machine research systems are often rewarded for useful completion:
produce the report, finish the search, generate the next experiment, answer the question.
A system can verbally acknowledge uncertainty while operationally building on top of it. The
critical question is whether uncertainty changes what may happen next. “Evidence is mixed” is
not a governed state if the system immediately proceeds as though one interpretation had
survived.
Claim inflation
Research transformations create repeated opportunities for scope and certainty to expand: source
 summary  synthesis  argument  conclusion. Each step may be reasonable individually, yet → → → →
the final claim can become stronger than the underlying evidence. Citation does not by itself
reveal how the transformation occurred.
Failure recovery versus failure discovery
Failure recovery asks how to continue. Failure discovery asks what the failure reveals about what
the inquiry believed. These are not equivalent. A failed experiment may indicate a bad method, an
unobservable variable, a wrong hypothesis, or a defective question. Treating all failure as an
obstacle to completion can destroy information.
5. Semantic Compression and Reality Primacy
The methodological basis for the architecture comes from a broader Governing Engines research
principle: a representation is not the thing represented. In What Is Intelligence?, the inquiry
explicitly treated conventional definitions as representations subordinate to whatever process
actually exists, sought competing explanations, and required contradiction to force revision.[1]
That paper defines abstraction failure as compression distortion becoming operational: a
compressed representation continues governing action after discarded structure becomes
consequential.[1] Research systems face the same problem. A transcript is a useful compression of
audio. A summary is a compression of a paper. A metric is a compression of a dataset. A citation
graph is a compression of scholarly relationships. The issue is not compression. The issue is
forgetting what was discarded.

[Source PDF page 8]
GOVERNING ENGINES  |  THE MASK AI RESEARCH WEARS  |  SEPTEMBER 2026
Reality Primacy: no representation, interpretation, model, or prior
state has authority to resist materially stronger evidence merely
because the existing state is coherent or already accepted.
Reality-Primacy challenge and revalidation
Reality Primacy requires a detection mechanism. Newly admitted evidence does not automatically
override prior state; it first challenges the parts of the inquiry that depend on what the new
evidence may invalidate. Revalidation is required when new evidence directly contradicts a load-
bearing observation or claim, invalidates an assumption used by the current interpretation,
exposes a material representation error, provides materially stronger or more direct evidence
than the evidence supporting the current state, changes a discriminating prediction, reveals false
source independence, invalidates the method by which current evidence was produced, expands
the relevant system boundary, or introduces evidence the current explanatory model cannot
represent.
The operational sequence is: Current State -> New Evidence Admitted -> Challenge Test ->
Counterfactual Materiality Test -> REVALIDATION_REQUIRED or NO_MATERIAL_CHALLENGE. If
revalidation is required, the affected state is reopened, competing interpretations are compared
against the evidence burden, and the state is either retained, narrowed, revised, superseded, or
left unresolved. The transition receives a receipt identifying the evidence, rationale, custody, and
affected objects.
Reality Primacy does not mean newest evidence wins. It means
materially challenging evidence can force accepted state back into
contact with its evidence burden.
Adjudication independence
The challenge and materiality tests create a second-order risk: the same reasoning process whose
state is being challenged could classify the challenge as immaterial and thereby protect itself from
revalidation. The architecture therefore separates detection from final adjudication for material
challenges. A primary research process may detect, classify, and recommend disposition; it may
not be the sole authority that finalizes an M2 or M3 challenge to its own accepted state.
Adjudication asks a narrower question than redoing the investigation: could the admitted
evidence plausibly change a valid downstream inquiry state, does it challenge a load-bearing
assumption, observation, representation, interpretation, or model, and is the proposed materiality
class defensible from the dependency structure of the inquiry?
If the primary process and adjudicator materially disagree, the state becomes
MATERIALITY_DISPUTED. The system must preserve the disagreement and apply the higher
plausible consequence class until review resolves it. Disagreement at the governance layer must
not be silently averaged away.

therefore not automatically independent adjudication.

PART III - RESEARCH INSIDE INSTITUTIONS
6. Public Research Surface
Frontier organizations may have unusually deep access to model behavior, telemetry, failures,
red-team results, unreleased experiments, and internal capability discoveries. Public papers,
model cards, safety reports, and benchmarks expose only some portion of that inquiry. This paper
calls the externally observable portion the Public Research Surface.
The concept is descriptive, not accusatory. Security, privacy, unfinished work, intellectual
property, contractual restrictions, and responsible-disclosure concerns can all justify limited
publication. The architectural issue is that external readers cannot infer the full internal inquiry
from the public artifact.
Who has custody of a finding?
A corporate researcher may be simultaneously a scientist, employee, builder, evaluator, and
adviser. Those roles create multiple authority relationships. A finding can be scientifically
material while its release remains governed by legal, security, or organizational processes. The
relevant systems question is therefore not to presume motive but to identify the chain of custody
between finding and institutional action.
Whistleblowing as a transmission event
Whistleblowing can be modeled as a failure or breakdown of ordinary research transmission:
Finding  Internal Transmission  Evaluation  Organizational Decision  Disagreement  → → → → →
Failed Resolution  External Transmission. The sequence does not establish why disagreement →
occurred. It does make the custody problem inspectable.

PART IV - INVESTIGATION OF EXISTING
SYSTEMS
7. Method of Comparative Investigation
The external investigation was deliberately adversarial to the paper’s emerging novelty claims.
Candidate gaps were treated as hypotheses to be attacked. When an existing system already
implemented a mechanism, the proposed gap was narrowed. The goal was not to prove that
Governing Engines had invented each piece. It was to determine what remained after prior art
and deployed practice were properly accounted for.
The comparison used primary product documentation, academic papers, open-source repositories,
standards, technical manuals, and selected public practitioner commentary. Consumer and
practitioner reports were treated as evidence of experience and friction, not as controlled
performance measurements. The investigation stopped at major-class saturation: additional broad
searches increasingly produced new examples of known mechanism families rather than new
architecture classes.
Comparative dimensions
A major methodological result of the investigation was the need to separate capabilities that are
often conflated.

Composite counterexample test
Testing products one at a time is not enough. A stronger adversarial question is whether a
competent team could compose existing mechanisms and close most of the claimed gap without a
new inquiry architecture. A plausible composite would combine W3C PROV for provenance, an
ELN-style immutable revision history, Scite-like support/contrast relations, ACH-style competing
hypotheses, ATLAS.ti-style source-segment interpretation, an agentic research system for
autonomous discovery, and event-sourced state history.

That composite would likely reproduce a large share of the local capability proposed in this paper.
The remaining problem is therefore not possession of the parts. It is whether those parts share one
authoritative evolving inquiry state, whether cross-function transitions have common semantics
and custody, whether question/model/interpretation changes propagate coherently, and whether
the resulting system remains broadly usable and accessible. This paper does not claim that such a
composite cannot be engineered; rather, the composite counterexample narrows the constructive
contribution toward integration and governing transition semantics.
8. Literature, Citation, and Review Systems
Elicit: structured review state
Elicit’s systematic-review workflow supports research-question refinement, gathering papers,
explicit screening criteria, screening recommendations, human overrides, data extraction, and
synthesis. Its public documentation emphasizes PRISMA-auditable exclusion reasons, per-criterion
scores, supporting quotes, and sentence-level citations.[2] This is substantial procedural
provenance. It does not, from the reviewed public documentation, establish a complete historical
record of how interpretations changed as the inquiry evolved.
Scite: support and contradiction at citation scale
Scite’s Smart Citations classify citation statements as supporting, contrasting, or mentioning and
expose citation context rather than relying on counts alone.[3] This directly disproves any claim
that contradictory-evidence discovery is absent from contemporary literature infrastructure. Scite
itself cautions that such classifications are navigation signals rather than proof of study quality
and encourages return to the cited context and paper.[3]
ResearchRabbit: discovery through scholarly relationships
ResearchRabbit attacks a different boundary: keyword dependence. Citation networks allow
researchers to move backward to foundational work and forward to later work that may extend,
challenge, or supersede earlier findings.[4] That demonstrates that research discovery can follow
relationships rather than only lexical similarity.
9. Agentic Research and Automated Science
Deep Research: autonomous trajectory
OpenAI describes Deep Research as an agentic capability that conducts multi-step research,
searches and analyzes large numbers of online sources, pivots as information is encountered, can
browse uploaded files, and presents a summary of steps and sources.[5] Its significance here is not
that it produces reports, but that it moves research direction autonomously during the run.
Robin: inquiry crossing into physical experimentation
FutureHouse describes Robin as an end-to-end scientific workflow integrating hypothesis
generation with experimental data analysis.[6] Robin matters because the research loop is not

confined to digital text: human-performed physical experiments can return data into a machine-

remaining part of one workflow.
Gemini Deep Research: multimodal grounding
Google’s 2026 Deep Research announcement explicitly supports grounding with PDFs, CSVs,

10. Laboratory, Qualitative, and Provenance Systems
Laboratory history
LabArchives automatically timestamps additions, deletions, and modifications and retains
immutable revision histories that can be inspected and reverted.[9] This establishes that strong
historical preservation is mature infrastructure. But its own documentation also shows that not
every activity is versioned: comments, tags, links, and some copy actions are excluded.[9] What a
system preserves is determined by what its architecture considers state.
Qualitative multimedia analysis
ATLAS.ti allows researchers to create exact audio/video quotations while listening or watching,
code those segments, add comments and first interpretations, play them back in context, and
capture video frames as separate image documents for further analysis.[8] This is a strong
precedent for linking original multimedia evidence to a derived interpretive structure without
reducing the entire source to a transcript.
Provenance standards
W3C PROV defines interoperable provenance in terms of entities, activities, and agents and is
intended to support questions about origin, trust, compliance, and reproducibility.[10] Provenance
itself is therefore a mature standards problem. The paper’s narrower concern is semantic
continuity of an inquiry across changes in interpretation, question, hypothesis, and model.
11. Intelligence, Forensics, and Investigative Systems
Competing hypotheses
Analysis of Competing Hypotheses is explicitly designed to reduce confirmation bias by requiring
analysts to consider alternatives and evaluate evidence against them. Controlled research
complicates any simplistic endorsement: Dhami, Belton, and Mandel found that trained analysts
did not follow all ACH steps, evidence for reducing confirmation bias was mixed, and the method
could increase inconsistency and error.[11] The lesson is important: explicit structure does not
guarantee correct execution.

Open forensic hypothesis governance
Forensia provides an especially relevant open-source precedent. Its documentation states that
language-model output is never stored directly as fact; actual evidence is searched and checked
first. It manages hypotheses through confirmed, refuted, inconclusive, untestable, and new-lead
states and distinguishes an empty query result from genuine refutation based on observability
and coverage.[12]
DFIR Companion goes further in another direction: it exposes evidence-linked findings, “ask the
case” queries, status-tracked hypotheses, contradicting evidence, explicit falsification review,
known unknowns, and investigation logs.[13] These systems directly disprove broad claims that
public investigative software lacks governed hypothesis state or adversarial evidence review.
Open investigative memory
Aleph is an open-source toolkit for investigative data analysis capable of searching and cross-
referencing heterogeneous structured and unstructured data.[14] Its public investigative
workspace supports uploaded documents, entities, network diagrams, and list comparison. OCCRP
describes its public service as helping users “build your investigative memory.”[14] The significant
distinction is that investigative memory can still be organized primarily around documents,
entities, and relationships rather than the full semantic evolution of interpretation.
Hypothesis to artifact
Timesketch states an explicit goal of helping analysts move from a hypothesis to forensic artifacts
that support or invalidate it.[15] This is another direct precedent for evidence-bound hypothesis
testing. It reinforces the central finding that the pieces exist; the challenge is continuity across
pieces.
12. Reasoning Provenance and Epistemic State
Recent agent research narrows the gap further. A 2026 paper on Reasoning Provenance for
Autonomous AI Agents argues that state checkpoints, execution traces, and telemetry do not
natively provide structured, queryable records of why an agent chose an action, what it inferred
from an observation, how the inference changed strategy, or which evidence supports the final
verdict. The proposed Agent Execution Record stores intent, observation, inference, versioned
plans, revision rationale, evidence chains, and authority context.[16]
This directly supports one of the paper’s strongest distinctions: preservation of state is not the
same as preservation of transition meaning. Yet reasoning provenance remains agent-centric. The
general investigator problem is broader: the inquiry can cross human action, machine action,
inaccessible evidence, multimedia, experiments, question revision, model revision, and
publication.

PART V - CONSTRUCTIVE ARCHITECTURE
14. Inquiry as the Persistent Primary Object
The constructive architecture begins by changing the unit of persistence. An Inquiry is not a chat,
report, document, search session, or project folder. Those can belong to it. The Inquiry is the
continuing research object that survives changes in question, evidence type, hypothesis,
interpretation, model, researcher, and agent.
Terminology discipline and prior-art mapping
The architecture does not require every familiar mechanism to be renamed. Provenance entities,
activities, agents, version events, audit records, and state transitions already have mature
representations in standards and deployed systems. Governing Engines terminology is retained
only where it adds inquiry-specific semantics rather than cosmetic vocabulary.

The architecture is constrained by invariants rather than by a single preferred workflow. Modes

Reality Primacy
Reality outranks representation. Evidence that passes the challenge and counterfactual materiality
tests can force affected state into revalidation; newer evidence does not win merely because it is
newer.
Materiality
Materiality is determined by counterfactual consequence to inquiry state. M2/M3 changes require
persistent state; M3 changes require explicit transition records and heightened review.
No Self-Finalizing Material Challenge
A reasoning path may detect and propose disposition of a challenge to its own state, but for M2/M3
challenges it may not be the sole authority that determines the challenge is immaterial, resolved,
or safe to ignore. Materiality adjudication must be sufficiently independent to expose self-
protective classification and must preserve MATERIALITY_DISPUTED when adjudicators disagree.
Adjudication Independence
Independence is a property of the review path, not merely the number of agent calls. The system
must record the adjudication basis and the form of independence used - separate reasoning pass,
distinct role, independent evidence path, different model, human review, or an approved
combination - so conformance can later be tested rather than assumed.
Observation - Interpretation Separation
Observation must not silently become interpretation.
Representation - Source Separation
Derived representation must remain traceable to the source and must not silently replace it.
Source-State Integrity
Discovered, located, accessible, inspected, and cited are different source states.
Evidence Existence
Inaccessible or unlocated evidence must not be represented as nonexistent.
Search-Scope Integrity
Conclusions must not imply a broader evidence search than actually occurred.
Adversarial Search
Material hypotheses must generate searches for evidence capable of weakening them.
False Independence
Repeated claims sharing one upstream origin must not be counted as independent corroboration.
Contradiction Preservation
Material contradiction may remain unresolved and must not disappear through synthesis.
No Silent Transition

18. Multimodal Evidence
The architecture accepts text conversion because language is often the most convenient reasoning
surface. It rejects the idea that the conversion becomes the entire evidence object.
For audio, the transcript captures what was said while the waveform and acoustic context may
carry pace, emphasis, laughter, hesitation, overlap, or environmental events. For video, frames
and motion can add posture, gesture, physical sequence, and surrounding context. These cues do
not automatically establish intent; they constrain interpretation. The system should therefore
preserve original evidence and derived representations together.
The practical model is: Original source  derived representations  observations  interpretation.→ → →
Each layer remains distinguishable and returnable.
19. Search Beyond the Internet
The relevant evidence environment is larger than the searchable web. A book listing can establish
that a book exists without establishing what the book says. A library catalog can identify a source
without turning its metadata into substantive evidence. An archive finding aid can expose a lead
without making the underlying records inspected.

[Source PDF page 22]

Accordingly, source state matters. DISCOVERED, LOCATED, ACCESSIBLE, ACQUIRED, INSPECTED,
PARTIALLY_INSPECTED, UNAVAILABLE, and INACCESSIBLE describe different realities. The
system should report sources discovered but not examined and create acquisition tasks when a
material evidence trail continues beyond machine access.
20. Material Conflict Escalation
Material Conflict Escalation is the mechanism by which contradiction becomes operational state
rather than decorative caveat. When a conflict can materially affect the governing question,
hypothesis, evidentiary sufficiency, claim scope, methodological validity, or conclusion, the system
should isolate the conflict and preserve the exact underlying evidence before progression.
A Conflict Packet should contain the competing claims or interpretations, exact source references,
the reason they conflict, variables or contexts that differ, affected finding or hypothesis, known
unknowns, and what evidence would discriminate among alternatives. Possible outcomes include
RESOLVED_A, RESOLVED_B, CONTEXT_DEPENDENT, REPRESENTATION_ERROR,
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, REQUIRES_NEW_TEST, QUESTION_REFRAMED, or
UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION.

The design objective is deliberately public: serious investigator/research capability should not
require law-enforcement status, university affiliation, enterprise procurement, or another
institutional gate. The comparative investigation shows that public and open-source systems can
already provide meaningful investigative functions. The harder problem is integration without

24. Findings by Evidentiary Status
Externally evidenced findings
 AI already performs material research functions across discovery, synthesis, experimentation,
multimodal analysis, and autonomous continuation.
 Provenance, contradiction detection, competing hypotheses, multimodal evidence, revision
history, and belief-revision mechanisms already have substantial prior art.
 Preserving state, presenting state, and governing state are distinct capabilities.
 Professional disciplines distribute epistemic controls differently: law emphasizes custody;
intelligence competing hypotheses; science reproducibility; qualitative research source-

 Users and practitioners often remain the cross-system integration layer even as local
automation increases.
 Current agent research increasingly treats reasoning provenance and epistemic state

Conceptual findings
 Research Custody: who or what controls material epistemic transitions.
 Epistemic Materiality: machine or human contribution is material when changing it could
alter the question, evidence set, hypothesis, interpretation, claim scope, conclusion, or
direction.
 Progression Bias: the structural tendency to convert unresolved states into actionable

 Oversight Compression: research throughput can expand faster than meaningful human
inspection capacity.
 Public Research Surface: the externally observable portion of an institution’s actual inquiry.
 Semantic Continuity of Inquiry: material changes in questions, evidence interpretations,
hypotheses, models, findings, and direction remain reconstructably connected over time.
Design findings
 The Inquiry should be the persistent primary object rather than the document, task, case, or
final answer.
 Observation, representation, interpretation, claim, hypothesis, finding, and transition require
distinct semantic states.
 Material state changes require explicit evidence, rationale, consequences, and custody.
 Search and evidence acquisition should remain part of inquiry state, including inaccessible but
discovered sources.

25. Research-Custody Disclosure for This Paper
Human-originated research object
The governing research problem originated with the human researcher. The initial concern was
not that AI should be excluded from research. It was that machine participation could expand
while conventional artifacts continued to obscure where material inquiry functions occurred.
Material machine participation
AI materially participated in organizing candidate sections, identifying comparable systems,
conducting public-source comparisons, retrieving documentation, surfacing related research
traditions, proposing distinctions, challenging overbroad novelty claims, drafting prose, and
integrating findings. Removing that participation would have changed the evidence set,
terminology, comparisons, and direction of later investigation. Describing the AI as merely a
writing tool would therefore understate its epistemic role.
Human custody
The human researcher retained custody over the originating research object, whether proposed
distinctions belonged in the inquiry, when the assistant was moving into design too early, the
requirement to investigate before architecture, the definition of the inquiry itself as the target
object, the need to search for evidence for and against hypotheses, the requirement to include
books, libraries, images, audio, video, and physical evidence environments, the accessibility
objective, the acceptance or rejection of conceptual formulations, and publication readiness.
Shared inquiry and revision
Some conceptual transitions were shared. The distinction between preservation and presentation
was sharpened through direct human correction of an AI characterization. The gap repeatedly
narrowed as external systems disproved broader claims: provenance was not missing;

[Source PDF page 27]
GOVERNING ENGINES  |  THE MASK AI RESEARCH WEARS  |  SEPTEMBER 2026
contradiction handling was not missing; hypothesis state was not missing; serious open
investigative tooling existed; epistemic-state architectures existed. The surviving claim became
narrower because the research changed the paper rather than the paper controlling the research.
Retrospective disclosure limitation
This paper did not originate inside the purpose-built inquiry-state system it proposes. Its custody
disclosure was reconstructed retrospectively from the conversational research process rather
than generated from canonical Transition Records, Conflict Packets, or evidence-bound Custody
Records. It therefore demonstrates the disclosure concept but does not demonstrate conformance
with the stronger audit standard proposed for future implementations. The limitation is material:
readers are being given an attributed reconstruction of custody, not a replayable machine-
auditable custody ledger.
Prospective standard
This disclosure is prospective and methodological. The paper does not claim that the proposed
Research-Custody Disclosure is already an established scientific standard, nor that older research
artifacts should be retroactively judged by a framework developed after their publication. Prior
artifacts should preserve their publication state. Future disclosures should focus on epistemically
material transitions, not every keystroke.
26. Limitations
Public documentation is not complete visibility
The comparative investigation relies on public documentation, public papers, open repositories,
standards, technical manuals, and selected practitioner commentary. A system may contain
internal mechanisms that are undocumented externally. Therefore “not established in the
reviewed public materials” must not be converted into “does not exist.”
Consumer reports are not controlled evaluation
Public user and practitioner commentary helped expose workflow friction, manual verification,
integration burden, and accessibility barriers. These reports are self-selected and context-
dependent. They are evidence of experience, not universal performance measurements.
The system landscape changes quickly
This comparison reflects the public system landscape examined through September 2026. New
systems may close gaps identified here. That would not invalidate the method; it would change the
research state.
Architecture is not validation
The proposed investigator/research system has not yet been fully built and evaluated as the
integrated system described. This paper therefore does not claim superior accuracy, lower

[Source PDF page 28]
GOVERNING ENGINES  |  THE MASK AI RESEARCH WEARS  |  SEPTEMBER 2026
hallucination, better science, better investigations, or better user outcomes. Those remain testable
predictions.
Materiality and Reality-Primacy mechanisms remain unvalidated
Version 1.2 closes the semantic ambiguity that previously left “material” and Reality Primacy
under-operationalized and adds an architecture-level adjudication boundary: materiality is
defined by counterfactual consequence to inquiry state; Reality Primacy has an explicit challenge-
and-revalidation trigger; and M2/M3 challenges cannot be self-finalized by the same reasoning
path whose state is under review. These mechanisms remain design hypotheses until
implementation shows that different operators and adjudicators can apply them consistently, at
acceptable cost, without turning the framework into procedural theater.
Adjudicator independence remains unvalidated
The architecture specifies proportionate independence, dispute preservation, and human custody
for governing transitions, but it does not yet establish which combinations of separate passes,
roles, models, evidence paths, and human review produce sufficient independence in practice.
Two agents using the same model and assumptions may reproduce the same blind spot. The
technical specification and evaluation program must therefore treat common-mode adjudication
failure as a first-class risk.
27. Falsification Conditions
Existing integrated counterexample
The central comparative finding should be narrowed or rejected if a mature, broadly accessible,
general-purpose system is demonstrated that already integrates evolving inquiry state,
interpretation/model/question lineage, multimodal and multi-environment evidence acquisition,
autonomous investigation, transition semantics, and complete inspectable lineage.
No measurable value from inquiry-state preservation
The constructive thesis should be weakened if controlled testing shows that preserving
interpretation history, transition rationale, contradiction, question lineage, and failed branches
provides no meaningful improvement in auditability, correction, reconstruction, error detection,
or human understanding compared with substantially simpler systems.
Governance causes more distortion than it prevents
The architecture should be revised if its controls systematically bias outcomes, suppress legitimate
exploration, produce false certainty, impose prohibitive procedural burden, or encourage
evidence to be forced into state categories. Structured analytic techniques already show that
adding structure is not automatically beneficial.[11]

Reality Primacy becomes rhetorical
If implementation cannot provide mechanisms by which evidence can reopen, challenge, revise,
or disconfirm accepted research state, then the system does not operationalize Reality Primacy. It
merely names it.
Adjudication collapses into self-confirmation
The adjudication architecture should be revised if the same reasoning process can routinely
classify challenges to its own state as immaterial without effective independent review, if
nominally independent adjudicators exhibit common-mode agreement that blocks justified
reopening, or if MATERIALITY_DISPUTED is routinely collapsed for throughput rather than
resolved against evidence.
28. Evaluation Program
The eventual implementation should be tested rather than admired architecturally. Useful
measures include unresolved-state preservation, contradiction retention, disconfirmation
sensitivity, claim-scope discipline, source-return reliability, interpretation-revision fidelity,
question-revision quality, false-independence detection, evidence-horizon accuracy, oversight
burden, semantic inquiry replay, materiality-classification agreement, adjudicator disagreement
rate, justified-reopening recall, and common-mode adjudication failure.
Comparisons should include more than a bare language model. Relevant baselines include
conventional web research, ordinary AI deep research, structured systematic-review workflows,
human-only research, and human-plus-AI workflows without explicit inquiry-state governance.
Failure will be informative. If users ignore the history, materiality classification fails, governance
produces excessive latency, machine rationales prove unreliable, or multimodal lineage becomes
prohibitively expensive, those outcomes can disconfirm assumptions in the architecture. A system
governed by Reality Primacy must itself remain subordinate to reality.

PART VII - CONCLUSION
29. The Mask Becomes Removable
The paper began with an appearance problem. Research can retain papers, citations, methods,
hypotheses, experiments, and conclusions while the location of inquiry inside that structure
changes. As machine systems acquire more epistemic functions, the familiar artifact becomes a
weaker proxy for the process that produced it.
The answer is not to prohibit machine research. Nor is it to preserve humans ceremonially.
Human judgment can fail, institutions can distort, and machine systems can legitimately perform
enormous amounts of useful inquiry. The integrity question is whether material transitions
remain locatable, evidence-bound, challengeable, and revisable.
The comparative investigation matters because it prevented an easy novelty story. Nearly every
local mechanism already exists somewhere: provenance, contradictory-evidence discovery,
competing hypotheses, chain of custody, multimodal interpretation, revision history, belief
revision, evidence graphs, autonomous research, physical experimentation, reasoning
provenance. The substantive claim that survives is narrower: these mechanisms can be composed,
but rigorous machine-mediated inquiry still requires one authoritative evolving inquiry state with
shared transition semantics, custody, and a non-self-finalizing path for material challenge.
The system should not merely return what it concluded. It should be
capable of returning how the inquiry became capable of concluding
it.
Under the proposed architecture, the paper becomes one view of a larger state. The underlying
inquiry can preserve failed paths, alternate interpretations, inaccessible evidence leads, search
history, question revision, contradiction, model change, and custody. The narrative can remain
readable because compression is allowed. What is not allowed is losing the ability to return to the
research state that the compression represents.
Final thesis
As AI systems assume increasing epistemic functions within
research, the integrity of inquiry will depend less on preserving the
appearance of continuous human authorship and more on
preserving identifiable custody, reconstructable lineage, and
independently challengeable adjudication across material research
transitions - including question formation, evidence acquisition,

[Source PDF page 31]
GOVERNING ENGINES  |  THE MASK AI RESEARCH WEARS  |  SEPTEMBER 2026
representation, interpretation, contradiction, hypothesis revision,
model revision, findings, and return to reality.
Constructive thesis
A general investigator/research system can be organized around the
evolving Inquiry as its persistent primary object, allowing human
and machine researchers to search, investigate, interpret, challenge,
revise, experiment, and publish while preserving evidence-bound
transition history under Reality Primacy and preventing material
challenges from being finalized solely by the reasoning path they
challenge.
The original question was: when machines increasingly perform the search and humans
increasingly judge the result, what remains human about research? The inquiry produced a more
fundamental one:
Who - or what - has custody of the inquiry while understanding is
changing?
That is the part conventional research artifacts increasingly fail to show. And that is the mask AI
research wears.
