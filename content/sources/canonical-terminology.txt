TERM:
CONTINUITY
==================================================

CANONICAL DEFINITION:
The preserved integrity between intent, interpretation, execution, consequence, memory, identity, and observable reality across time and system transitions.

OPERATIONAL PURPOSE:
Prevent fragmentation and preserve coherence across participating systems.

ALLOWED OPERATIONAL CONTEXTS:
- governance systems
- execution systems
- narrative systems
- cognitive systems
- social systems
- institutional systems

PROHIBITED INTERPRETATIONS:
- static repetition
- rigid sameness
- frozen behavior
- symbolic consistency without structural integrity

RELATED TERMS:
- interpretation
- lineage
- memory
- consequence
- stewardship

DEPENDENCY RELATIONSHIPS:
Continuity depends on:
- lineage preservation
- stable interpretation
- bounded drift
- reviewable transitions
- operational memory

GOVERNANCE NOTES:
Continuity is the foundational trunk principle beneath all Governing Engines systems.

DRIFT RISKS:
- fragmentation
- interpretive mutation
- continuity collapse
- false coherence
- lineage loss

INHERITANCE RULES:
Derivative systems may specialize continuity domains but may not remove continuity-preservation requirements.
