SECTION 1
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
==================================================

Modern AI systems increasingly participate directly inside:
- operational workflows,
- enterprise execution chains,
- orchestration systems,
- governance systems,
- decision infrastructures,
- and consequence-generating environments.

Most organizations currently govern:
- outputs,
- runtime behavior,
- observability,
- and post-execution auditing.

This is insufficient.

Operational instability often emerges BEFORE runtime execution occurs.

Execution instability is frequently the downstream expression of:
- unstable terminology,
- unstable interpretation,
- unstable authority,
- unstable continuation,
- unstable proof,
- unstable operational boundaries,
- and ungoverned semantic transitions.

Therefore:

The enterprise governance problem is no longer solely:
“Did the system execute correctly?”

The problem becomes:

“Can the organization prove operational meaning, authority, execution legitimacy, continuation validity, and governance integrity remained admissible before consequence propagation occurred?”
