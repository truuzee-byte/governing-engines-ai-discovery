==================================================
PURPOSE
==================================================

This document establishes Mechanism Primacy as a
foundational governing principle of Governing Engines.

Mechanism Primacy defines how governed systems
shall be understood before they are modified.

The purpose of this doctrine is to ensure that
system improvements target the mechanisms
producing observed outcomes rather than reacting
to symptoms or isolated results.

==================================================
DEFINITION
==================================================

Mechanisms produce outcomes.

Outcomes do not explain themselves.

Before modifying a system, the mechanism
responsible for producing an observed outcome
shall first be identified.

==================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Reality reveals outcomes.

Mechanisms produce outcomes.

Governance improves mechanisms.

==================================================
GOVERNING LAW
==================================================

No modification shall be justified solely by an
observed outcome.

Every proposed modification shall be supported by
an identified mechanism explaining why the outcome
occurred.

==================================================
REASONING MODEL
==================================================

Most reasoning proceeds directly from observation
to conclusion.

Mechanism Primacy proceeds through mechanism
discovery before modification.

Observation

↓

Pattern

↓

Mechanism

↓

Diagnostic

↓

Constraint

↓

Design

↓

Validation

==================================================
MECHANISM LAW
==================================================

A mechanism shall explain:

• why an outcome occurs

• how an outcome occurs

• under what conditions the outcome occurs

• what constraints govern the outcome

• what changes will alter future outcomes

==================================================
PATTERN LAW
==================================================

Patterns suggest the existence of mechanisms.

Patterns alone do not explain behavior.

Repeated observations shall initiate mechanism
discovery rather than immediate intervention.

==================================================
DIAGNOSTIC LAW
==================================================

Diagnostics exist to expose mechanisms.

Diagnostics do not exist to produce conclusions.

Understanding shall precede intervention.

==================================================
DESIGN LAW
==================================================

Governed systems shall be improved by modifying
mechanisms rather than treating symptoms.

Changing symptoms affects isolated outcomes.

Changing mechanisms changes future outcomes.

==================================================
GOVERNANCE LAW
==================================================

Governance shall evaluate proposed changes
according to their effect on governing mechanisms
rather than isolated results.

Mechanism understanding shall precede
authorization of system modification.

==================================================
RELATIONSHIP TO REALITY PRIMACY
==================================================

Reality Primacy determines whether a claim is
supported by evidence.

Mechanism Primacy determines what mechanism
produced the observed outcome.

Reality Primacy answers:

"Is this claim supported by evidence?"

Mechanism Primacy answers:

"What mechanism produced this outcome?"

The two principles operate together.

Reality establishes what is true.

Mechanism explains why it is true.

==================================================
RELATIONSHIP TO DIAGNOSTICS
==================================================

Diagnostics expose mechanisms.

Mechanism Primacy governs how those mechanisms
are interpreted.

Diagnostics provide visibility.

Mechanism Primacy provides direction.

==================================================
RELATIONSHIP TO MODE
==================================================

Mode observes.

Mode identifies patterns.

Mode proposes candidate mechanisms.

Diagnostics evaluate those mechanisms.

Reality Primacy validates claims regarding those
mechanisms.

Builder constructs governed improvements to the
identified mechanisms following operator
authorization.

==================================================
APPLICATION
==================================================

Mechanism Primacy applies to:

• enterprise diagnostics

• runtime governance

• system architecture

• organizational design

• process improvement

• software engineering

• construction systems

• AI governance

• operational analysis

No governed system shall be modified without first
identifying the mechanism responsible for the
observed behavior.

==================================================
FINAL LAW
==================================================

Outcomes reveal that something happened.

Mechanisms explain why it happened.

Governed systems improve mechanisms,
not symptoms.
